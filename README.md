Analysis:
Although there are a good amount of upgrades for the boss, the fundamental 4 attacks don’t change much. Activate ability, aim at player, repeat. 


Player AI also is limited. Only jump height increases and a new projectile attack is added as game progresses.


Also imo it should be harder to land attacks and harder to dodge attacks, like in eldenring/terraria where the player keeps dodging ur big attacks and chips ur health away.


Conclusion:


To tackle repetitive gameplay, the following could be added:
* Player should be a human (multiplayer): AI is limited in how it can adapt to your attacks (sounds like a pain in the ass to code) so a real person (comes with built in AI called the “brain” or smt?) could be on the other side.
* Adaptive AI: If multiplayer is too much of a pain in the ass, we should try to make an AI that can detect and identify boss attacks and react accordingly. Perhaps the detection and reaction time will change as the player dies and “learns” more about the attacks. AI could also get better at using its weapon over time, such as better aim (less spread, starts accounting for movement?) for non homing ranged weapons or being able to hit you perfectly with the far edge of its weapon hitbox for melee weapons to avoid getting hit itself.
* Multiple bosses: after inevitably losing to the adaptive player, the player unlocks new gear (more movement/weapon options) and you become the next boss in progression (with new attacks and mechanics). You get to pick the next boss u become and the boss u are affects what upgrades the player gets when they kill you.
* * Dynamic arena: player could construct a custom, destructible(by certain boss attacks) arena for every fight like terraria. Allows for a degree of strategy/adaptation. 


Other ideas:

Currently im thinking of a platformer like mario with enemies and a timer (and speed determines the quality of loot u get) but im not sure yet.
